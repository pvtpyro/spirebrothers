# Huffman, The Arbitrator: card plan (draft for review, 2026-10-27)

Huffman (a friend named Joshua; always "Huffman" in the mod) is the law, and he brought his dog and a production line.
Darktide main: **Arbitrator** (Adeptus Arbites, very Judge Dredd; a cyber-mastiff that attacks on command). Also **Total
Annihilation** (metal flows in, the Commander builds everything), **Dyson Sphere Program**, and **modded Minecraft** tech
packs (AE2 storage, jetpacks). He was the office IT guy who worked with Daniel and Brandi: servers, email, the boss's
purchase orders. John is his nephew, but they're the same age and grew up together.

He's a homebody who hates to travel, will never use a public bathroom (he always waits until he gets home), and loves fried food.

**Built 2026-10-27, untested in game.** Changes while building: the starter **Turn It Off and On Again** is **Have You Tried Rebooting?** (Daniel already has that card), **Jetpack** is **Jetpack Boost** (David has a Jetpack), **Blue Screen** is **Blue Screen of Death**, **Build Queue** makes your next Build need no Metal (instead of costing 0), **Tear Through** and **Lockdown** Mark their own target, and **Judge, Jury, and Executioner** deals 24, doubled on a Marked enemy. First set: 5 Basics, 70 reward cards (20 / 34 / 16), and 2 Duo cards. Grows later.
Numbers are first-pass balance based on vanilla (Strike 6, Defend 5). Visuals: his pixel body, borrowing the
Necrobinder's vanilla scenes (the one vanilla character no brother uses yet, and the one with a pet, Osty).
Dredd flavor uses original lines only.

## His mechanics

| Keyword | Rule |
|---|---|
| **The Mastiff** | His cyber-mastiff stands at his feet the whole combat (a pet, untargetable like Tim's Kids). It has a **Bite** number (starts at 3). **At the end of your turn, the Mastiff bites the Marked enemy** (a random enemy if none is Marked) for its Bite damage. Cards raise Bite for the combat. *(Its name is a placeholder until we hear his Darktide dog's name.)* |
| **Mark** | A debuff that tells the Mastiff who to bite. Only one enemy is Marked at a time: Marking another moves it. |
| **Law** | Power cards that set a rule for the fight. When an enemy breaks it, it's punished right away. The enemy's own moves set them off, which nobody else in the mod does. |
| **Metal** and **Build X** | Total Annihilation. **Metal** is a counter on Huffman that builds up during combat (from Extractors and other cards). **Build X:** when you play the card, if you have X Metal, spend it to get the Build bonus. Without the Metal the card still works, just without the bonus. |

Shared mechanics he also uses: **Share** (co-op support) and **Duo**.

## Starting deck (11 cards) and relic

- 4 Strike, 4 Defend
- **Sic 'Em** (Basic Skill, 1): Mark an enemy. The Mastiff gains 2 Bite.
- **Turn It Off and On Again** (Basic Skill, 0): Discard your hand, then draw that many cards. *(The IT guy's first answer to everything.)*
- **Nanolathe** (Basic Skill, 1): Gain 4 Block. Gain 3 Metal.
- **Starter relic: Arbites Badge.** The first Power you play each combat costs 0. *(Laws are Powers.)*
  **Touch of Orobas upgrade: Judge's Badge**: the first 2 Powers.

Legend: 🐕 Mastiff, ⚖️ Law, 🏭 Metal / Build, 💻 IT, 🤝 co-op.
