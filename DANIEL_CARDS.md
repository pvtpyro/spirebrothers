# Daniel: card expansion (built 2026-10-03)

52 new cards, bringing his reward pool from 20 to 72 (vanilla characters have 75 to 84).
All 52 are built. Small changes made while building: Appease the Machine Spirit also draws 1 card (so it is never a dead card), Overclock upgrades to Retain, Cover Me! upgrades to 8 Block, and most cards upgrade by +2 to +4 to their main number.

Legend: 🛡 = gives Block (or Dexterity/Plating). Tags: **L** = Logic, **H** = Hands.
Numbers are first-pass balance, based on vanilla (Strike 6, Defend 5, roughly 8 damage or Block per energy for a Common).
