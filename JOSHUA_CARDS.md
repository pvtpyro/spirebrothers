# Joshua, The Musician: card plan

Piano, guitar, vocals. A ballad guy, not a shredder. Lived in Japan for a while; now back home in the US.
In his own words (2026-10-26): favorite games are "probably Slay the Spire or oldschool 2D Zelda games", and he's "bigger into D&D or board games". Also PlateUp!, Terraria, Stardew Valley, and Archipelago (the multiworld randomizer, archipelago.gg).
He does **not** like Rocket League much (that's Tim's game) and he **doesn't like playing support**.

**Ballad pass 2026-10-26, untested in game.** He's now the frontman: slow songs that wear enemies down (Heartstrings)
and patience that pays off in one huge Chorus (Held Note). Healing is down to 5 cards; Share cards are gone.
Numbers below match the code. Cut, rename, or rewrite anything, especially to add inside jokes.

## His mechanics

| Keyword | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Song** | Playing this card gives you **1 Verse**, shown as a note orb floating above Joshua (max 10). |
| **Chorus** | **Spends all your Verses** for an effect scaled by Verses spent. Choruses now help **Joshua himself** (or hit enemies), not ALL players. |
| **Held Note** | Verses still there at the **start of your turn** become **Held** (they glow) and **count double** for a Chorus. Build quietly, then one big finish. |
| **Heartstrings** | Enemy debuff: **whenever a Song is played, it loses HP** equal to its Heartstrings (ignores Block). Goes down by 1 at the end of its turn. |
| **Roll** | D&D: roll a d20 (run RNG, shown over his head). **1 = Fumble** (half effect), **20 = Natural 20** (double). **Advantage** rolls twice and keeps the higher. Lucky rerolls the first 1 each turn; Dungeon Master adds +4; the **Lucky d20** relic (Common) makes the first Roll each combat a 20. |
| **Check** | Archipelago: find a random **item** and send it to a **random player** (yourself in solo). See the item table below. |
| **Tribute** | Two very personal Rares: **Pastor** and **Coming Home** (kept as his few team heals). |

## Build notes (for Claude)

- New core: a `VersePower` counter on Joshua, a `Song` keyword that adds 1 Verse after the card is played,
  and a `Chorus` helper that reads and spends all Verses (respecting Bridge, Encore (spends half), and Choir).
  Show Verses the way Daniel's Train of Thought works.
- "Add a random Song card" (Improvise, Junimos, Mixtape): BaseLib `CommonActions.GenerateCards` with a
  filter for the Song keyword.
- **Thousand Cranes** and **I'm Not Done Yet** share the Lizard Tail death-prevention hooks; Thousand Cranes
  must watch every player, not just the owner.
- **Zero-Second Goal**: a playability check like Stratagems (hand count is 1).
- **Check**: roll the item with the run RNG (`Owner.RunState.Rng`) and pick the receiving player the same way, so co-op stays in sync. Items go through vanilla `BlockNextTurn`-style powers or direct Block, `DrawCardsNextTurnPower`, and `EnergyNextTurnPower`, the same as Daniel's Share cards. Scouting stores a "next Check is Progression" flag in a small power. BK Mode uses a playability check like Stratagems.
- Healing in co-op targets other players' creatures, the same as Want Some? and Stratagem: Reinforce.

## Balance pass (2026-10-08)

Goal: no brother or card underpowered; only effects that scale without limit were toned down.

- **Encore:** the first Chorus each turn spends half your Verses (rounded down) instead of none, so Verses no longer stay at 10 forever.
- **Big Finish:** 5 -> 3 damage per Verse (4 upgraded), below Rock Opera.
- **Well-Worn Guitar:** 2 -> 3 starting Verses (Signature Guitar 4 -> 5), so his first Choruses aren't empty in solo.
- **Check:** Filler items give 6 Block instead of 4. **Release!** costs 1 (was 2). **Multiworld** costs 1 (was 2; 0 upgraded).
- **Nice Shot!:** now a Song (it was the same card as Daniel's Common Spaghetti Code).

## Attack pass (2026-10-25)

The guitar now gives Verses every turn, so he almost always has 3. Verse thresholds went from 3+ to 5+ (Aerial, Guitar Feedback).
Metronome became an attack, and 5 new attacks were added (Copper Shortsword, Glissando, Taiko Drums, Guitar Solo, Zenith):
22 attacks in the reward pool now, 77 reward cards in total (22 / 38 / 17).

## Ballad pass (2026-10-26)

Josh doesn't like playing support, doesn't really play Rocket League, and is a ballad guy, not a shredder.

- **New mechanics:** Heartstrings (`Powers/HeartstringsPower.cs`, triggers in its own `AfterCardPlayed` on any Song)
  and Held Note (`VerseOrb.Held`, set for all Verses in `JoshuaTracker.AfterPlayerTurnStartEarly` so the guitar's Verse
  arrives fresh; `Verses.ForChorus` adds `HeldCount * HeldBonus`; Encore spends the newest Verses first, so Held ones stay).
- **Retired** (set to `CardRarity.Basic` so they never show up in rewards, shops, transforms, or random generation, but
  saved runs that have them still load, like Hum a Tune): Lend an Ear, Boost Pad, Harmonize, Watering Can, Plant Parsnips,
  Cherry Blossom Picnic, Omiyage, What a Save!, Aerial, Nurse, Heart Crystal, Comfort Food, Green Tea, Care Package,
  Community Center, Duet, Kickoff, Demo, Calculated., Nice Shot!, Hymn, Harvest Festival, Jam Session, Zero-Second Goal.
- **Reworked in place:** Sing Along, Matsuri, Karaoke Night, Standing Ovation, Harmony (now self / offense).
- The Duo cards (Hype Man, Babysitting, Minor Key) still help the partner; that's their point.

## D&D and Zelda pass (2026-10-26)

Josh named his favorites: Slay the Spire, old-school 2D Zelda, D&D, and board games (no specific ones, so none are in).
- **Roll** (`Mechanics/Dice.cs`): `Dice.Roll` uses `RunState.Rng.CombatTargets` for co-op sync and shows a speech bubble
  (`SPIREBROTHERS-DICE.roll/nat20/fumble` in powers.json). Roll attacks use `JoshuaCard.RollAttack` + the `RollMultiplier`
  CalculatedDamage multiplier. Powers: `AdvantagePower`, `LuckyPower`, `DungeonMasterPower`; relic `LuckyD20`.
- Ballad of the Wind Fish uses `Chorus(ctx, keepVerses: true)`: the Chorus counts (and runs listeners) but spends nothing.
  A Chorus that spends 0 no longer uses up Encore or Bridge.
- Deleted (never released, so no saved run has them): Deep Breath, High Note, Humming, Opening Number, Showstopper,
  Sheet Music, Nocturne, Final Note.
